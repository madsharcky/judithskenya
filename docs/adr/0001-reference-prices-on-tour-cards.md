# Reference prices on tour cards

The client wants price honesty — "the total cost" is one of the first questions her customers ask, and research shows Oribi's price-led cards work — but RECAP has no confirmed prices yet, and the design skill (design-taste-frontend) forbids invented prices on a service page. Decided, on the client's explicit instruction overriding the skill: tour cards and detail pages show "from" prices sourced from similar regional operator tours (Oribi, Spoton, Bonfire and peers), each price with its source logged in the prototype report.

**Considered options**

- No prices, quote-first cards — rejected: the price-honesty pattern and the card layout cannot be reviewed without a price slot.
- Invented prices — rejected: they would read as real and misquote the business.
- Regional reference prices (chosen): research-grounded stand-ins, clearly logged, every one replaced with a confirmed RECAP price before launch.

**Consequences**

Prices are stand-ins like the photographs: the prototype report lists every price with its source, and the pre-launch checklist must swap each for a client-confirmed number.
