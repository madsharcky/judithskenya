# RECAP Tours and Travel website

Domain language for the RECAP Tours and Travel site: tour offerings, how bookings work, and the business brand. Terms come from the client brief (docs/customer/to-questionnaire-judiths-kenya-tours.md).

## Language

**RECAP**:
The business brand the site presents — RECAP Tours and Travel, the client's registered business. The site communicates the business, not the guide personally.
_Avoid_: "Judith's Kenya Tours" (early working name), personal branding

**Tour**:
An offering a customer can book: either a Package or a Custom tour.
_Avoid_: trip, excursion, product

**Package**:
A standard tour with a fixed itinerary, duration and inclusions, sold as listed.
_Avoid_: fixed tour

**Custom tour**:
A tour tailored to a specific client's preferences — accommodation, activities or dates.
_Avoid_: bespoke tour

**Category**:
One of the four groupings of tours on the site: Wildlife safaris and national parks; Lake, nature and adventure; Coastal Kenya and beach holidays; Cultural, historical and unique experiences.
_Avoid_: destination, tour type

**Joining tour**:
A shared departure where the group size is set by vehicle capacity.
_Avoid_: group tour, shared tour

**Private tour**:
A tour for a single client or party — solo, family or corporate.
_Avoid_: exclusive tour

**Deposit**:
The payment required to confirm a booking.
_Avoid_: down payment, advance

**Payment link**:
A Stripe-hosted checkout link the business generates per customer — tied to the customer's email or phone — so the customer can pay a deposit by card without an account.
_Avoid_: invoice, payment page

**Admin panel**:
The private area of the site that only the RECAP owner can access, used to generate payment links for customers and to review site analytics.
_Avoid_: back office, dashboard

**Driver-guide**:
The professional who drives and guides on tours.
_Avoid_: guide (where the driving role matters)
